# MemeRetrieve business model and first paid pilot

English · [简体中文](../business-model.md) · [Product overview](README.md)

This proposal is dated 28 November 2026. The proposed first customer is a content operator who reuses their own animations and often needs the exact original. Start with one operator per library, such as an independent creator managing a brand account or a small studio’s asset manager. Recruit people who have at least 100 GIFs they can use, look for originals at least three times a week, and control a tools budget. People who only need a similar reaction can often use the search built into their chat app. Their needs can be tested separately.

## Evidence and alternatives

The web app imports GIFs, saves originals under each account, searches private collections by screenshot, and downloads the original animation. It can find a saved asset again offline after a restart. In a fixed library of 12 GIFs, a frame taken about one third of the way through each file placed the target in the top three in 12/12 cases with multi-frame indexing and 11/12 with first-frame indexing. In an online browser test, a description and a source-site clue recovered a 44-frame Earth GIF. It ranked first online, then first again offline after collection. Both downloads matched the reference byte for byte. Six earlier attempts are preserved in the record. Real chat screenshots, crops, occlusions and missing targets require separate evaluation. ## Initial offer

Test a seven-day trial with assistance importing the customer’s own GIFs. The proposed **HK$99 monthly plan** includes one hosted private library, one operator, 2 GB of originals, local-mode screenshot search and downloads. In local mode, “local” refers to the server that runs retrieval. A hosted customer uploads files to that server. For the pilot, use dedicated deployments and handle metering, payment and support manually. The existing rehearsal runs library-only search on the operator’s computer. A hosted trial requires checks on access, outbound network policy and costs on its own host. A brand asset manager could purchase the service and be its first operator. Team sharing, role approvals and cross-customer libraries require separate validation. [Beta rehearsal](../guides/public-beta.md) · [Product deployment guide](../guides/product-deployment.md)

Customers supply the initial library and confirm they can have it hosted, searched and downloaded. The team’s Noto examples retain the creator, source, licence and modification history. Keep customer uploads in their own private library and check the applicable permissions before any cross-customer display or distribution. GIPHY’s integration rules prohibit storing copies of GIPHY media from its API, so the proposed hosted library would use other sources. If customers later request online recovery, review each source’s storage and download conditions, limit fetching to approved sources, and confirm commercial provider access, consent for sending screenshots and usage limits. The course gateway quota was used for historical engineering tests. ## Contribution per subscription

Keep accounts in Hong Kong dollars. Start with monthly subscription and add-on receipts net of refunds. Subtract payment fees, media storage and backups, requests and transfer, allocated retrieval and database costs, vision, search and language-model calls, and support labour. Allocate free-trial usage to customer acquisition. Convert foreign charges at the settled rate and record the billing month. Track ongoing development, sales and other fixed expenses separately.

For example, suppose one account pays HK$99. Assume HK$3 in payment fees, HK$12 for storage, backups, compute and database, and five minutes of support at HK$120 per hour (HK$10). Monthly contribution is **HK$74**, about 75% of revenue. At 20 minutes of support, it falls to HK$44, about 44%. Allocate the full cost of each dedicated instance, including idle time. At HK$74 per month per account, 100 paying accounts would cover HK$7,400 of other monthly fixed costs before acquisition. Replace these assumptions with bills and recorded staff time.

Track online recovery separately. Divide the external calls, fetching and compute costs of **all** jobs in a batch, including failures and retries, by the number of original recoveries confirmed by users. If nobody finds the target, report total spend and zero recoveries. DeepSeek’s published prices distinguish inputs, outputs, cache use, standalone search and search invoked by the model. Its standalone text search price on the research date was US$0.75 per 1,000 successful requests. Obtain a commercial quote for the vision service separately. Cloudflare R2 Standard provides a storage comparison at US$0.015 per GB-month plus operations. The current installation uses persistent volumes. ## Six-week decision

| When | Experiment | Decision criteria agreed in advance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Recruit 10 eligible participants from creator communities and 10 through small-studio referrals. Each brings four screenshots with known targets in their own library and one whose target is absent. Balance tasks across their existing method and MemeRetrieve. Randomize order and assignment so a person never sees the same task twice. | Record outreach, eligibility, trial starts and recruitment time by channel. Each method gets 40 in-library tasks and 10 absent-target tasks. With MemeRetrieve, at least 32/40 in-library originals should be found and played within 60 seconds, with at most one absent target falsely identified in 10 trials. Cap timeouts at 60 seconds, retain failures for both methods, and require a median time reduction of at least 30%. |
| Week 2 | After the seven-day trial, quote the same HK$99 price to all 20 participants. Record non-activation, payments, refunds and reasons for declining. | An activated user imports at least 30 originals and independently finds and downloads one on a different day. Out of all 20, at least 10 should activate and five should pay for the first month. Record stated willingness to pay separately. |
| Weeks 3–6 | Track unprompted retrievals across weeks, staff time and bills. Check for a second payment after each customer’s first month ends. For disputed matches, the asset owner checks the original file and the team keeps the decision and elapsed time. | At least 60% of first-month payers make a second monthly payment (at least three if five paid initially). Require at least a 60% observed contribution margin. Divide each channel’s recruitment labour, promotion and trial costs by net new paying customers. Divide acquisition cost by actual monthly contribution per customer to get the payback period. Target three months or less. |

If quality, payment, renewal and unit economics meet the thresholds, use the channel with the shorter payback period for a second round of 50 eligible people. If people save time but do not renew, test a one-time installation or migration service with a new group. If the target is frequently missed, improve retrieval and import first. If support or idle hosting consumes the contribution, change the delivery cost before recruiting again. The 20-person trial is sized to inform the next investment decision.
